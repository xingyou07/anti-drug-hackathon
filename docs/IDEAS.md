# Ideas

Brainstorm for the anti-drug hackathon, grouped by who each idea serves. Chosen ideas are marked ✅.

## For kids and teens

- ✅ **Choose-your-path story game.** An interactive party scenario where choices branch into realistic consequences. *Shipped as "The Party".*
- ✅ **Peer-pressure practice partner.** A pushy friend the kid practices saying no to, with feedback. *Shipped as "Say No Practice".*
- **"What's really in it?" scanner.** Photograph or type a vape, pill, or drink and get a plain-language explanation, with warnings about counterfeit pills laced with fentanyl.
- **Future-you simulator.** A respectful, non-gory health progression visualization plus a "what I'd lose" panel (sports, savings, relationships).

## For people struggling with addiction

- ✅ **Craving rescue button.** A guided 10-minute flow: breathing, grounding, a distraction game, a reminder of why they quit, and a text to a trusted contact. *Shipped.*
- ✅ **Recovery streak and money-saved tracker.** Days clean, money not spent, milestones. *Shipped.*
- **Judgment-free chat companion.** An AI companion for late-night moments. Needs guardrails: no medical advice, crisis detection, hotline surfacing.
- **Nearby help finder.** A map of rehab centers, support groups, and hotlines, with an "I'm not ready to quit yet" path. *Partially shipped as the Help tab (hotlines only).*

## For parents, teachers, and the community

- **Slang and emoji decoder.** Paste a message and learn what it might mean, with non-defensive conversation starters.
- **Story wall.** Anonymous, moderated recovery stories on the theme "what I wish I'd known at 15." Needs moderation, so it's a stretch goal.

## Why this combination

Judges reward a clear target user, a working demo, and something emotionally memorable. Combining the story game, the refusal practice, and the craving button gives two audiences in one app, with the interactive pieces demoing well in seconds.
